Frequently Asked Question

You May Ask

What services does PhilGen Constructions offer?
We specialize in construction, architectural & structural design, project management, renovations, road & pavement construction, interior & exterior finishing, landscaping, steel fabrication, electrical & mechanical installations, and water & drainage systems. Whether it’s residential, commercial, or industrial, we’ve got you covered.
How do I get a quote for my project?
You can request a free consultation by contacting us via phone, email, or our website contact form. We’ll discuss your project requirements and provide a detailed quotation based on scope, materials, and labor.
How long does a construction project take?
The timeline depends on the size, complexity, and type of project. Small renovations may take a few weeks, while larger residential or commercial constructions can take several months. We provide a project schedule after the initial consultation.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes, PhilGen Constructions is a fully licensed and insured construction company. We adhere to industry regulations and safety standards, ensuring legal compliance and peace of mind for our clients.
Do you handle government projects and tenders?
Yes, we have experience working on government and private sector projects, including infrastructure development, public buildings, and civil works. We follow all tendering procedures and compliance requirements.
Can you help with design and approvals?
Absolutely! Our architectural and structural design team prepares drawings, blueprints, and 3D renderings. We also assist in submitting plans for county approvals, permits, and compliance documentation.
What type of materials do you use?
We use high-quality, durable, and locally or internationally sourced materials depending on project requirements. Our focus is on strength, sustainability, and cost-efficiency.
Do you offer maintenance and after-construction support?
Yes! We provide post-construction support, maintenance services, and renovations to keep your property in top condition.
How do I ensure my project stays within budget?
We work with clients to develop a realistic budget and offer cost-effective solutions without compromising quality. Our project managers keep everything on track with regular updates and financial transparency.
What payment options do you accept?
We accept various payment methods, including bank transfers, mobile money, and installment payment plans for eligible projects. Payment terms are outlined in our agreements.
